KRA iTax & eTIMS Compliance Standard

To operate seamlessly in the Kenyan jurisdiction, Tryphene Murugat Consultancy standardizes all fixed-price Phase 3 milestones and T&M Discovery retainers through QuickBooks Online, automatically mapped to the eTIMS API.

1. Chart of Accounts Mapping

  • Revenue Account: `4010 - Consulting Services (Software Architecture)`
  • Current Asset: `1200 - Withholding Tax Receivable (5% KRA)`
  • Liability: `2100 - Subcontractor Payables (Devs/Analysts)`

2. The 5% Withholding Tax (WHT) Rule

When billing corporate clients (e.g., Apex SACCO), they are legally required to withhold 5% of your gross professional fee and remit it directly to KRA.

Example: Phase 3 Tranche 1 (KES 400,000)

1. You issue eTIMS Invoice for KES 400,000 (Gross).
2. Client pays KES 380,000 (Net cash via RTGS).
3. Client issues you a KRA WHT Certificate for KES 20,000.
4. Bookkeeper Action: Credit Accounts Receivable (400k). Debit Bank (380k). Debit WHT Receivable (20k).

3. Subcontractor Dispatch & Taxation

  • When paying the UI Lead or DB Architect, you must withhold 5% from their payouts and remit to KRA via iTax by the 20th of the following month.
  • Subcontractors must provide their KRA PINs before mobilization. No PIN = No Work Order.
