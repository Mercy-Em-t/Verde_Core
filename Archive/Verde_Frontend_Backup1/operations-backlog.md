# Operations & Scaling Backlog

This backlog contains the operational and business scaling tasks defined in the *Client Onboarding and Defect Reduction* PDF playbook. These items are scheduled for implementation after the core commercial platform software (Tryphene Platform v5) is completed.

## 1. Subcontractor Operations
*   **Contractor Task-Board Setup:** Configure JIRA/Linear/ClickUp boards.
*   **Ticket Templates:** Create standardized ticket structures with strict acceptance criteria for subcontracted Backend and UI specialists.

## 2. Finance & Compliance
*   **eTIMS Invoicing Automation:** Standardize invoicing workflows to comply with Kenyan tax laws.
*   **Tax Accounting Automation:** Automate Chart of Accounts and 5% Withholding Tax (WHT) deductions between QuickBooks and KRA iTax.

## 3. Client Communications
*   **Client Reporting Pack:** Create standardized templates for weekly sprint progress memos and burn-down reports.
*   **Document Digitization:** Digitize the *Stakeholder Discovery Intake Questionnaire*, *Phase 1 Acceptance Form*, and *Scope Freeze Agreement* into the platform's Template Library.

## 4. Client Onboarding
*   **Client Kickoff Session:** Design the post-signing orientation meeting agenda to ensure the client feels in control without being overwhelmed.
