## 1. Engagement Framework & Hybrid Billing Structure

Services are segmented into sequential, standalone phases. Work progresses strictly upon written client sign-off and funding of the respective phase.

*   **Phase 1 (Project Initiation & Feasibility): Time & Materials Retainer**
    *   **Billing Rate:** [e.g., KES 300,000 – 400,000] per 1-week sprint.
    *   **Terms:** Billed and paid 100% upfront before sprint commencement.
    *   **Core Deliverables:** System Request, Feasibility Analysis (Technical, Economic, Organizational), Initial Risk Matrix, Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), and Project Charter.
*   **Phase 2 (Requirements Analysis): Time & Materials Retainer**
    *   **Billing Rate:** [e.g., KES 350,000 – 500,000] per 1-week sprint (estimated at 2 to 4 weeks depending on stakeholder availability).
    *   **Terms:** Billed weekly in advance. Unused hours do not roll over; active discovery pauses immediately if a weekly sprint invoice remains unfunded.
    *   **Core Deliverables:** Formal Requirements Definition, Use Cases, Process Models (Data Flow Diagrams), and Logical Data Models (ERDs).
*   **Phase 3 (System Architecture & Technical Design): Fixed-Price Milestone**
    *   **Total Fixed Fee:** [e.g., KES 800,000 – 1,200,000]
    *   **Payment Tranches:**
        *   40% Initiation Deposit: Due upon signing Phase 3 Work Order.
        *   30% Midway Delivery: Due upon delivery of User Interface Wireframes/Prototypes and Architecture Specifications.
        *   30% Final Sign-Off: Due upon final handover of Physical Database Schemas, Program Specifications, and System Specification Document.
*   **Phase 4 (System Implementation & Deployment): Fixed-Price Milestone**
    *   **Total Fixed Fee:** [e.g., KES 1,500,000 – 2,500,000]
    *   **Payment Tranches:**
        *   40% Initiation Deposit: Required to mobilize software development agents and infrastructure.
        *   30% Feature Freeze / Test Readiness: Due upon test plan execution, module verification, and deployment to staging.
        *   30% Final Handover & Deployment: Due upon User Acceptance Testing (UAT) sign-off, system cutover, and post-implementation audit delivery.

## 2. Prerequisite Phase Gates (Anti-Scope Creep Policy)

To maintain quality and avoid unbilled analytical overhead:
1.  **No Direct Builds:** The Manager will not accept an engagement starting directly at Phase 3 or Phase 4 unless the Client provides comprehensive, verifiable prerequisite documentation (Signed Requirements Definition, DFDs, ERDs, and Program Specs).
2.  **External Document Audits:** Any documentation supplied by a third party or the Client is subject to a mandatory, billable Discovery & Specification Audit (1-week minimum T&M retainer) before being accepted as baseline documentation for Phase 3 or Phase 4.
3.  **Scope Freeze:** All requirements defined in Phase 2 become immutable once signed. Any modification requested during Phases 3 or 4 triggers a separate Change Request evaluated at the standard weekly T&M rate.

## 3. Role of the Manager & Subcontracted Specialists

*   The Client acknowledges that the Manager acts as engagement director and technical orchestrator. Specialist analysts, designers, and software engineers engaged during any phase are managed directly by the Manager.
*   Direct technical requests or task alterations issued by the Client to subcontracted personnel without the Manager's written consent are null and void.
