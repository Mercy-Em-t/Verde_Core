PART 2: CONVERSION & CHANGE MANAGEMENT

1. System Conversion Strategy

Migrating from the legacy paper-based ERP to the new OmniLogix cloud platform.

  • Conversion Style: Phased Rollout (Branch by Branch). Rationale: Mitigates risk by isolating potential system failures to a single depot before scaling nationally.
  • Data Migration: Legacy stock balances exported via CSV and mapped/seeded into the new PostgreSQL TBL_ITEM schema on Friday at 22:00 EAT.
  • System Go-Live Date: Target Cutover is Monday 06:00 EAT. Legacy system becomes Read-Only.

2. Change Management Plan

Managing the human element and organizational friction during cutover.

  • Training: Interactive mock-receiving sessions held in the cafeteria using the actual Zebra TC21 scanners.
  • Management Sponsorship: The COO must send a mandatory company-wide memo enforcing the cutoff of the old paper system. Shadow IT (keeping parallel Excel sheets) is strictly forbidden.
  • Incentive Alignment: Floor clerks given performance bonuses for achieving 99% scan accuracy during the first 14 days of Phase 4 Go-Live.
