1. Workshop Purpose & Executive Mandate

The objective of this 4-hour working session is to systematically define and align cross-departmental business processes across Procurement, Warehouse Operations, Finance/Accounts Payable, and IT.

Our engineering teams do not write code based on assumptions. The consensus reached during this session directly dictates the Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs), Relational Schemas (ERDs), and the immutable System Proposal for Phase Gate 2.

3. Working Ground Rules & Facilitation Protocol

To complete our agenda within the 4-hour timebox, all participants adhere to four rules:

  • Rule 1: Focus on Business Logic, Not Technical Implementation. Discuss operational triggers, data rules, and exception cases. Do not debate database syntax, programming languages, or server configurations.
  • Rule 2: Solve for the Organization, Not the Silo. Workflows will be evaluated against primary project KPIs: reducing the 14% discrepancy rate and shortening dock receiving from 4 hours to 45 minutes.
  • Rule 3: Decouple Contested Handoffs. Where departmental needs conflict (e.g., dock turn speed vs. invoice precision), we will implement decoupled intermediate states (e.g., 'Gate-In' vs. 'Reconciled').
  • Rule 4: The 24-Hour Executive Escalation Rule. Unresolved operational deadlocks will be logged in the Conflict Register and escalated to the Chief Operating Officer for a binding determination within 24 hours.

  • • Hour 2 (10:00 - 11:00): Floor Receiving, Scanning, & Physical Exception Handling
  • • Hour 3 (11:00 - 12:00): Stock Reconciliation, Ledger Commits, & Finance GRN Matching
  • • Hour 4 (12:00 - 13:00): Data Entity Review, Conflict Sign-Off, & Phase 2 Next Steps
